Safety Tips

Task Board connects you with people in your area, but coordination happens off-platform. A few tips to stay safe:

Meet in public first

For your first interaction with someone new, consider meeting at a public landmark (a plaza, church, mosque, or busy junction) rather than a private address, especially if the task doesn't require them to come to your home.

Verify before you commit

Ask for a phone call before meeting in person. A short conversation can help confirm the person is legitimate.

Share your plans

Let a friend or family member know who you're meeting, where, and roughly when you expect to be done.

Trust your instincts

If something feels off about a request or a person's behavior, it's okay to decline or walk away.

Agree on pricing upfront

Discuss and agree on cost before work begins, to avoid disputes afterward.
