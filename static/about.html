About Task Board

Task Board is a simple, local notice board built to solve a everyday problem: finding someone reliable nearby to help with small jobs — fixing a generator, tailoring, tutoring, plumbing, and more.

Why we built this

In most communities, finding a trustworthy provider for small tasks happens through word-of-mouth or scattered WhatsApp groups. Task Board makes it easier to post what you need and find people offering that skill nearby — all in one place.

How it works

Anyone can post a task describing what they need done. Providers browse the board, find tasks that match their skills, and reach out directly using the contact info provided. The actual coordination — pricing, scheduling, meeting — happens off-platform, over phone or WhatsApp, just like it already does today.

Built for real communities

This board was designed with categories relevant to everyday needs — from generator repair to dispatch riders to event planning — reflecting the kinds of tasks people actually post and need help with.
