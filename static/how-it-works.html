How It Works

1. Post a task

Fill out the form on the Home page with what you need done, your contact info, a category, and your general location. You can also add a nearby public landmark if you'd feel more comfortable meeting there.

2. Providers browse and reach out

People offering that kind of service browse open tasks, filter by category, and contact you directly using the phone number you provided.

3. Coordinate off-platform

Once someone reaches out, you handle pricing, scheduling, and logistics directly over phone or WhatsApp — just like you already would.

4. Mark it as taken

Once you've found someone for the job, mark the task as "Taken" so others stop reaching out about it.

5. Edit or delete anytime

Made a mistake, or the task's details changed? You can edit or delete your posted task at any time from the Home page.
